# Fictional brand example — replace during setup

This example is invented for a product called **Northstar Notes**. It is a writing aid only and contains no facts about the installing team.

## Voice

Clear, direct, and calm. Use short sentences. Describe what a person can do and the evidence for a claim. Avoid inflated phrases such as “revolutionary” and unsupported superlatives.

## Sample

Before: “Our groundbreaking intelligence layer supercharges every team.”

After: “Northstar Notes groups project decisions in one place. In this fictional example, a teammate can find the latest decision by topic.”

During `/pb-setup`, replace this with approved brand rules, examples, and prohibited claims from the user's repository, connected sources, local files, or direct answers. Do not copy the fictional sample into customer-facing output.
