# 3. Protect the denominator

Define numerator and denominator in words and SQL. Decide whether the unit is an event, user, account, session, or opportunity. Specify eligibility, bot/test exclusions, identity stitching, time zone, and observation cutoff. Report both counts beside every rate. Check duplicate events and late arrivals.

For a fictional activation rate, use “distinct eligible users who activated within seven days” over “distinct eligible users who signed up in the cohort.” Do not use all historical signups as the denominator for a seven-day cohort.
