## Recommended branch

Pursue **A**, using a human-prepared digest and distinguishing genuinely actionable gaps from watch-only items. F1 has the strongest independent support in this limited fixture, and the pilot can test the timing mechanism without building matching. B is the runner-up: if C1/C2 receive earlier notice but cannot recruit because availability is inaccurate, availability becomes the upstream bottleneck and B takes priority. E supplies a guardrail, preventing a false “earlier is better” victory.

Two-week feasible pilot: six consenting fictional coordinators; a human prepares one digest for each upcoming eligible event. No automated assignment or volunteer contact. Proposed decision: at least four of six coordinators make one recorded replacement request within 48h of receiving an actionable gap, with no more than one reporting unnecessary outreach induced by the digest. The small pilot probes feasibility and mechanism; it cannot establish broad cancellation reduction. Actual cancellation measurement follows only after baseline/control and exposure checks.
